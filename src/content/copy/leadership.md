## JOHN JOAQUIN — FOUNDER

**Title:** Founder & CEO

> **⚠ Review:** The live site says "Founder & CEO". Press releases say "Founder". The Master Plan describes an unpaid CEO role during startup. Confirm the title. See audit §3.6.

**Short bio:**
John is a service-driven leader who has spent his career helping organizations and communities move forward with clarity and purpose. After decades guiding complex initiatives in technology, government, and economic development, he turned his focus to Southeast Volusia — a community he has been part of for more than 35 years — at a historic moment of opportunity. Known for uniting partners and stepping up when leadership is needed, John founded the Florida Space Innovation Center to ensure the region's growth creates real, lasting value for local families and future generations.

**My Story:**
I grew up a farmer kid in Northern California, working in the rice, walnut, and almond fields my family has tended since they arrived in the state in 1867. That upbringing shaped everything about me — the work ethic, the humility, the belief that you pitch in wherever you're needed, and the understanding that strong communities are built by people who show up for one another.

That sense of service eventually pulled me across the country to Washington, D.C., where I spent decades working in technology and government consulting, helping organizations solve problems and strengthen the systems people rely on every day. Along the way, I found myself drawn again and again to education and community leadership — serving on school boards, supporting workforce initiatives, and stepping into roles where I could help create opportunities for others.

Life brought me to Central Florida, where my wife grew up, and over time Southeast Volusia became home. After more than 35 years connected to this community, I've come to believe we are standing at a historic moment — a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to shape our future in a way that creates real value and lasting benefit for local families. And I also saw clearly that if we didn't act with purpose, our community could miss out on the benefits this moment can bring.

That realization — and a lifelong commitment to serving where I'm needed — is what led me to help create the Florida Space Innovation Center. FSIC is my way of bringing people together, filling the gaps that hold us back, and helping this community seize the opportunity in front of us. I believe deeply in Southeast Volusia and in what we can accomplish when we lead with intention and work together.
