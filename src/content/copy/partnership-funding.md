## WHY PARTNER WITH FSIC

**Headline:** Three Core Benefits for Florida Stakeholders

- **Ecosystem Building & Convening Power** — FSIC aligns schools, employers, civic organizations, and industry partners around shared priorities — reducing fragmentation and accelerating progress.
- **A Platform for Significant External Funding** — By demonstrating readiness, coordination, and long-term strategy, FSIC positions Florida to compete for federal, philanthropic, and private-sector investment at a scale individual organizations cannot secure alone.
- **Closing Gaps in Workforce, Talent, and Innovation** — FSIC strengthens the talent pipeline, supports innovators, and helps local companies grow — ensuring Florida has the skilled workforce and innovation capacity required for the next generation of space and technology industries.

> **Source:** fsic.space/news, May 25, 2026 launch release (verbatim)

## A PROVEN FOUNDATION

**Body:**
FSIC is built on approaches that have already delivered measurable results in other regions. It draws on the coordinated innovation model that propelled the quantum ecosystem in the Washington, D.C. area and the structured talent and industry strategy that reshaped Osceola County's semiconductor sector. These proven frameworks demonstrate how communities can organize, align partners, and attract major outside investment — and FSIC adapts these lessons to Florida's rapidly expanding space economy.

## FUNDING STRATEGY

**Headline:** [TODO — e.g. "How Is FSIC Funded?"]

**Body:**
FSIC uses a proven public-private funding model that blends:
- Philanthropic and private investment
- State and federal workforce grants
- Space Florida and economic development programs
- Earned revenue from training, partnerships, events and other activities.

Long-term projections show significant external funding potential aligned with FSIC's mission and program areas.

## GET INVOLVED

**Headline:** [TODO — e.g. "How Can the Public Get Involved?"]

**Body:**
FSIC is designed to be a community-powered movement, and there are many ways for residents, educators, businesses, and civic partners to participate from the very beginning.

Residents can get involved through:
- Community STEM nights that bring families together around hands-on learning
- Public exhibitions and arts-and-space events that celebrate creativity and innovation
- Student programs and camps that spark curiosity and build early skills
- Volunteer and mentorship opportunities supporting learners, educators, and small businesses
- Employer councils and advisory groups that help shape training, innovation challenges, and regional strategy

In addition, FSIC is actively seeking leaders and volunteers with relevant experience across the Center's four core engines — Talent, Innovation, Market, and Civic. Individuals with backgrounds in STEM education, engineering, advanced manufacturing, entrepreneurship, arts and culture, workforce development, community engagement, or related fields are encouraged to step forward. These early contributors will help shape programs, guide partnerships, and build the foundation for FSIC's long-term impact.

> **Source:** fsic.space FAQ, "How can the public get involved?" (verbatim)
