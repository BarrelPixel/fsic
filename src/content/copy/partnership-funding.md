## 01 · WHY PARTNER WITH FSIC

**Headline:** Three Core Benefits for Florida Stakeholders

- **Ecosystem Building & Convening Power** — FSIC aligns schools, employers, civic organizations, and industry partners around shared priorities — reducing fragmentation and accelerating progress.
- **A Platform for Significant External Funding** — By demonstrating readiness, coordination, and long-term strategy, FSIC positions Florida to compete for federal, philanthropic, and private-sector investment at a scale individual organizations cannot secure alone.
- **Closing Gaps in Workforce, Talent, and Innovation** — FSIC strengthens the talent pipeline, supports innovators, and helps local companies grow — ensuring Florida has the skilled workforce and innovation capacity required for the next generation of space and technology industries.

> **Source:** fsic.space/news, May 25, 2026 launch release (verbatim)

**A proven foundation (label column):**
FSIC is built on approaches that have already delivered measurable results in other regions. It draws on the coordinated innovation model that propelled the quantum ecosystem in the Washington, D.C. area and the structured talent and industry strategy that reshaped Osceola County's semiconductor sector. These proven frameworks demonstrate how communities can organize, align partners, and attract major outside investment — and FSIC adapts these lessons to Florida's rapidly expanding space economy.

> **Source:** fsic.space/news, May 25, 2026 launch release (verbatim). Published by FSIC, so it can be reused as published.
> **⚠ Review:** "Delivered measurable results" for the D.C. quantum and Osceola semiconductor examples isn't sourced in the PDFs. Fine as FSIC's own published statement; don't add figures about those regions.

## 03 · FUNDING

**Headline:** How Is FSIC Funded?

**Lead:** FSIC uses a proven public-private funding model that blends:
- Philanthropic and private investment
- State and federal workforce grants
- Space Florida and economic development programs
- Earned revenue from training, partnerships, events and other activities.

> **Source:** fsic.space FAQ, "How is FSIC funded?" (verbatim)

**Body:** The Florida Space Innovation Center is designed with a diversified, sustainable funding model that aligns with national best practices for nonprofit innovation hubs. The strategy balances philanthropy, public funding, employer partnerships, earned revenue, and federal grants. This approach reduces risk, strengthens long-term sustainability, and positions FSIC to scale responsibly as regional demand grows.

> **Source:** Master Plan p. 25 (verbatim).

**Florida disclosure** (under the tax status, and in the footer): shown automatically once FSIC's Florida charitable-solicitation registration number is set (`ORG.floridaRegistration`, `src/lib/site.ts`; `SolicitationDisclosure`). A COPY OF THE OFFICIAL REGISTRATION AND FINANCIAL INFORMATION MAY BE OBTAINED FROM THE DIVISION OF CONSUMER SERVICES BY CALLING TOLL-FREE (800-435-7352) WITHIN THE STATE. REGISTRATION DOES NOT IMPLY ENDORSEMENT, APPROVAL, OR RECOMMENDATION BY THE STATE." **⚠ Review:** counsel confirms the wording when the number is added.

**Tax status** (after the body, set off by a rule): Florida Space Innovation Center Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 43-2277165). Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

## 04 · GET INVOLVED

**Headline:** How Can the Public Get Involved?

**Lead:** FSIC is designed to be a community-powered movement, and there are many ways for residents, educators, businesses, and civic partners to participate from the very beginning.

**Residents can get involved through:**
- Community STEM nights that bring families together around hands-on learning
- Public exhibitions and arts-and-space events that celebrate creativity and innovation
- Student programs and camps that spark curiosity and build early skills
- Volunteer and mentorship opportunities supporting learners, educators, and small businesses
- Employer councils and advisory groups that help shape training, innovation challenges, and regional strategy

**Leaders and volunteers:** In addition, FSIC is actively seeking leaders and volunteers with relevant experience across the Center's four core engines — Talent, Innovation, Market, and Civic. Individuals with backgrounds in STEM education, engineering, advanced manufacturing, entrepreneurship, arts and culture, workforce development, community engagement, or related fields are encouraged to step forward. These early contributors will help shape programs, guide partnerships, and build the foundation for FSIC's long-term impact.

> **Source:** fsic.space FAQ, "How can the public get involved?" (verbatim)
> **⚠ Review:** The FAQ promises "a simple way to express interest in volunteering, advising, or serving in a leadership capacity." Until the Contact page has a form, the page's button opens an email to info@fsic.space. See `contact.md`.
