## A REGION AT AN INFLECTION POINT

**Headline:** A Region at an Inflection Point

**Body:**
The Space Coast is now the busiest launch corridor in the world, with commercial, civil, and defense missions driving rapid expansion in spacecraft manufacturing, robotics, autonomy, and dual-use technologies.

Yet Southeast Volusia lacks the coordinated systems needed to capture this opportunity. Without intervention, we risk absorbing population growth without gaining the high-wage jobs and employer investment that should accompany it.

> **Source:** fsic.space/about-4 (verbatim). "Busiest launch corridor" is supported by Master Plan p. 4.

## A NATIONAL SHIFT TOWARD PLACE-BASED INNOVATION

**Headline:** A National Shift Toward Place-Based Innovation

**Body:**
Federal and state policy is moving decisively toward regional innovation ecosystems. Billions in funding — CHIPS and Science Act, EDA Tech Hubs, NSF Engines, and state programs — are flowing to regions that can demonstrate coordinated capacity across workforce development, applied research, entrepreneurship, enabling infrastructure, and cross-sector governance.

The Space Coast has no civilian-facing innovation hub capable of competing for these investments. Southeast Volusia can fill that gap — but only if we organize now.

> **Source:** fsic.space/about-4 (verbatim); Master Plan p. 4.
> **⚠ Review:** "Billions in funding" needs a citation or softer wording. The Master Plan also lists "Build Back Better", which is omitted here (probably on purpose). See audit §3.5.

## A ONCE-IN-A-GENERATION CATALYST

**Headline:** Southeast Volusia County: A Once-in-a-Generation Catalyst

**Body:**
Southeast Volusia County is poised for transformative growth, with more than 20,000 new homes and over 12 million square feet of industrial and commercial space planned across the region. This scale of development in Edgewater, New Smyrna Beach and Daytona Beach will reshape our workforce needs, employer mix, and long-term economic trajectory.

**Without FSIC, Volusia County risks becoming:**
- a logistics corridor
- a low-wage service economy
- a commuter community for neighboring counties

**With FSIC, Volusia County becomes:**
- a magnet for high-value employers
- a hub for space and advanced manufacturing
- a destination for innovation, education, and community engagement
- a regional anchor for economic mobility and identity

> **Source:** fsic.space/about-4 (verbatim).
> **⚠ Review:** These figures (20,000 homes, 12M sq ft, region-wide) conflict with the Master Plan and the older draft (Deering Park: 10,000+ homes, 10M+ sq ft). Choose one framing and cite it. See audit §3.1 and §3.3.

## THE WINDOW IS OPEN

**Headline:** The Window Is Open — But Not for Long

**Body:**
The next five years will determine whether Southeast Volusia becomes:
- a full participant in the Space Coast's next decade of growth, or
- a region that watches opportunity flow past it

FSIC is designed to meet this moment. It provides the coordinated talent, innovation, commercialization, and civic infrastructure required to compete for high-value jobs, federal investment, and long-term economic mobility.

**Callout:**
FSIC transforms fragmented potential into coordinated progress. It ensures that opportunity flows into Southeast Volusia — not past it.
