## WHAT THE SITE COLLECTS

**Label:** In short: very little.

- **No cookies.** The site sets no cookies and has no accounts or sign-ins.
- **No forms.** The site has no contact, newsletter or donation forms. To reach FSIC, you email info@fsic.space.
- **No analytics, for now.** The site doesn't measure visits. If FSIC adds website analytics, this policy will name the service and what it collects before it's turned on.
- **No outside services on our pages.** The site's fonts and images are served from the site itself, so reading a page doesn't send your information to other companies.
- **Server logs.** Like any website, the site's host keeps standard server logs, which include visitors' IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. [TODO: name the host and link its privacy statement once hosting is final; today it is GitHub Pages.]

## WHEN YOU EMAIL FSIC

When you email info@fsic.space, FSIC uses your name, address and message to reply and to follow up on what you asked about. FSIC doesn't sell or rent your information, and shares it only when you ask us to (for example, to connect you with a partner) or when the law requires it.

To ask what FSIC holds about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email info@fsic.space.
