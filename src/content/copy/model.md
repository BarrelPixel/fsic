## TALENT ENGINE

**Shift line:** Transforming individuals into skilled talent

**Phase:** Launches first

> **Source:** Shift line from home.md (approved), Four-Engine Model cards. Phase from home.md (approved), Phased Deployment body.

**Summary:**
A coordinated, employer-validated workforce pipeline aligned to launch operations, spacecraft manufacturing, advanced manufacturing, robotics, and dual-use technologies.

Includes: micro-credentials, dual enrollment, industry-embedded learning, teacher upskilling, K–12 STEM exploration, and a Regional Talent Marketplace.

**Overview:**
The Talent Engine is FSIC's workforce powerhouse, building clear, employer-aligned pathways into the space and advanced technology sectors. It delivers hands-on STEM exposure, technical training, micro-credentials, internships, apprenticeships, and university pathways that prepare residents for high-wage careers. By connecting students, educators, and employers through a unified regional system, the Talent Engine increases credential attainment, boosts job placements and wage growth, strengthens employer satisfaction, and ensures that local talent can build their futures right here in Southeast Volusia County.

**Brings together:** Students · Educators · Employers

**What it solves:**
- Fragmented talent pipelines
- Lack of space-specific training
- Insufficient hands-on learning
- Limited employer access to local talent
- Weak alignment between education and industry

**Regional impact:**
- Increases the supply of job-ready talent
- Reduces hiring friction for employers
- Strengthens regional competitiveness
- Expands economic mobility for residents
- Positions Southeast Volusia as a workforce hub for the Space Coast

The region's first civilian-facing applied innovation hub.
Enables students, educators, researchers, startups, and industry partners to collaborate on real aerospace and advanced technology challenges.

The Innovation Engine turns regional challenges into new ideas, prototypes, and ventures that strengthen the Space Coast economy. By aligning innovation activity with employer needs and regional priorities, the Innovation Engine increases commercialization, accelerates new venture creation, expands university–industry collaboration, and ensures Southeast Volusia becomes a launchpad for space-focused innovation.

**Brings together:** Industry · Universities · Startups · Community partners

**What it solves:**
- No civilian innovation hub on the Space Coast
- Limited applied research capacity
- Lack of shared prototyping and testing facilities
- Weak industry-university collaboration
- No platform for dual-use technology development

**Regional impact:**
- Accelerates R&D and commercialization
- Strengthens university partnerships
- Attracts federal innovation funding
- Builds a culture of problem-solving and experimentation
- Positions Southeast Volusia as a hub for dual-use innovation

## MARKET ENGINE

**Shift line:** Transforming opportunity into market success

**Phase:** Scales as partnerships deepen and demand grows

> **Source:** Shift line from home.md (approved), Four-Engine Model cards. Phase from home.md (approved), Phased Deployment body.

**Summary:**
Strengthens the regional supplier base by helping companies secure certifications, navigate SBIR/STTR funding, access federal procurement pathways, and scale into sustainable ventures.
Ensures innovation generated in the region becomes market-ready and economically impactful.

**Overview:**
The Market Engine is FSIC's commercialization and business-readiness platform, helping local companies enter aerospace and defense supply chains, win contracts, and scale new ventures. It provides supplier-readiness training, certification support, SBIR/STTR navigation, startup acceleration, and direct connections to buyers, primes, and investors.

By aligning business support with regional industry demand, the Market Engine increases contract wins, strengthens local companies, attracts new employers, and ensures Southeast Volusia businesses can compete and grow in the space economy.

**Brings together:** Local companies · Buyers and primes · Investors

**What it solves:**
- Local companies locked out of supply chains
- Lack of certification and compliance support
- No regional accelerator or incubator
- Limited access to capital
- No structured pathway to federal procurement

**Regional impact:**
- Strengthens the local supplier base
- Increases contract wins and federal funding
- Supports startup formation and growth
- Attracts employers seeking a ready ecosystem
- Diversifies the regional economy

> **Source:** What it solves and Regional impact: Master Plan pp. 9–11, engine tables (verbatim).
> **⚠ Review:** "Brings together" is drawn from the overview above and is new for the page. The page shows Brings together, What it solves and Regional impact in place of the overview paragraph.

## CIVIC ENGINE

**Shift line:** Transforming innovation into shared identity

> **⚠ Review:** The Master Plan (p. 11) and the Overview PDF call this "Transforming Trust into Civic Legitimacy". home.md (approved) and the page use "Transforming innovation into shared identity". Confirm which one FSIC wants.

**Phase:** Scales as partnerships deepen and demand grows

> **Source:** Shift line from home.md (approved), Four-Engine Model cards. Phase from home.md (approved), Phased Deployment body.

**Summary:**
Builds public trust, regional identity, and community engagement through STEM events, arts and space programming, public exhibitions, and transparent governance.
Ensures innovation is visible, accessible, and meaningful to residents.

**Overview:**
The Civic Engine is FSIC's community-anchoring platform, building public trust, regional identity, and broad participation in the space economy. It connects residents, educators, employers, artists, and civic leaders through programs that make space, science, and technology accessible to all. By strengthening community engagement, expanding STEM culture, and aligning cross-sector partners, the Civic Engine builds long-term support for economic diversification, higher-wage opportunities, and a shared vision for Southeast Volusia's future.

**Brings together:** Residents · Educators · Employers · Artists · Civic leaders

**What it solves:**
- Lack of public understanding of the space economy
- Weak regional identity
- Limited community access to STEM experiences
- Insufficient civic engagement
- Need for visible public benefit

**Regional impact:**
- Builds community pride and belonging
- Strengthens civic trust and transparency
- Expands access to STEM opportunities
- Creates a shared narrative around regional growth

## GUIDING PRINCIPLES

**Headline:** Guiding Principles

**Statement:** Partner-first, gap-filling, and coalition-driven.

**Lead:** FSIC positions itself as a neutral convener that strengthens—not competes with—existing institutions by aligning talent, industry needs, and community priorities.

**Points:**
- The strategy starts small, delivers visible early wins, and scales only when partners signal readiness.
- Every partnership is structured for low operational burden, high shared value, and clear governance.
