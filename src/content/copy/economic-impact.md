## PLAIN-LANGUAGE SUMMARY (from current FAQ)

Independent economic modeling shows that FSIC dramatically improves the region's long-term economic trajectory. With full implementation over 25 years, FSIC is projected to help the region generate over $10 billion in economic output, support tens of thousands of job-years, increase local wages by more than $1 billion, and significantly expand fiscal revenue for local governments. It also positions the region to attract hundreds of millions in external funding tied to workforce development, innovation, and research.

> **Source:** fsic.space FAQ, "What economic impact will FSIC have on the region?" (verbatim). Useful as a lead-in. "Dramatically" goes against the tone guidelines.
