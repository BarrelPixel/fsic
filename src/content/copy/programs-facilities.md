## PROGRAM PORTFOLIO

**Headline:** Program Portfolio and Core Offerings
**Intro:** The Florida Space Innovation Center delivers a comprehensive portfolio of programs across the Talent, Innovation, Market, and Civic Engines. Each offering is designed to meet a specific regional need, align with employer demand, and create measurable impact for students, educators, businesses, and the community.

One row per engine: the engine's line, then its programs (each opens to its description). Each row links to that engine on the Model page.

**Talent Engine:** The Talent Engine provides a coordinated, employer-validated workforce pipeline that prepares students and adults for high-demand careers in aerospace, advanced manufacturing, robotics, and dual-use technologies.
- **Micro-Credential Academies:** Short, stackable credentials aligned to employer needs, including: robotics & automation; composites & materials; avionics fundamentals; precision machining; additive manufacturing; launch operations support. These credentials provide rapid pathways into high-value careers.
- **Dual-Enrollment STEM Pathways:** Partnerships with state colleges and universities to offer: engineering technology; advanced manufacturing; computer science and cybersecurity; aerospace technology. Students earn college credit while gaining hands-on experience.
- **Industry-Embedded Learning:** Programs that place students in real industry environments, including: job shadowing; internships; apprenticeships; employer-led workshops. These experiences strengthen career readiness and employer relationships.
- **Teacher Upskilling & Professional Development:** Training for educators in: emerging technologies; industry-aligned curriculum; hands-on lab instruction; STEM pedagogy. This expands regional teaching capacity and improves program quality.
- **Regional Talent Marketplace:** A digital platform that connects: students; job seekers; employers; educators. The marketplace provides visibility into regional opportunities and strengthens talent alignment.

**Innovation Engine:** The Innovation Engine provides the region's first civilian-facing applied innovation hub, enabling collaboration among students, educators, researchers, startups, and industry partners.
- **Challenge Labs:** Teams work on real aerospace and advanced manufacturing problems provided by employers, universities, or federal partners. Outcomes include prototypes, research findings, and new technologies.
- **Rapid Prototyping Studio:** A hands-on fabrication environment equipped with: 3D printers; CNC machines; electronics benches; materials testing tools. This space supports rapid iteration and applied learning.
- **Innovation Sprints & Hackathons:** Short, intensive events that bring together: students; engineers; designers; entrepreneurs. These events build community, accelerate problem-solving, and generate new ideas.
- **Applied Research Partnerships:** Collaborations with universities and federal agencies to support: dual-use technology development; autonomy and AI research; materials and manufacturing innovation; space systems and sensors. These partnerships strengthen regional competitiveness for federal funding.

**Market Engine:** The Market Engine supports local businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs through commercialization, supplier readiness, and federal funding pathways.
- **Supplier Readiness Bootcamps:** Training and support for companies seeking to enter aerospace and defense supply chains, including: quality systems; compliance requirements; contracting; procurement pathways.
- **Certification & Compliance Support:** Assistance with: AS9100; ITAR; CMMC; ISO standards.
- **SBIR/STTR Navigation:** Guidance for small businesses pursuing federal innovation funding, including: topic identification; proposal development; partner matching; post-award support.
- **Startup Acceleration:** Programs that support early-stage companies through: mentorship; business model development; access to capital; prototyping resources.
- **Contract Matchmaking & Procurement Support:** Connections to: prime contractors; federal agencies; spaceport operators; local government procurement.

**Civic Engine:** The Civic Engine ensures that innovation is visible, accessible, and meaningful to the public. It builds community trust, regional identity, and broad-based support for innovation-driven growth.
- **Community STEM Nights:** Hands-on events for families and youth that build excitement around science, technology, and space.
- **Arts + Space Integration:** Programs that blend creativity and technology, including: public art installations; space-themed exhibitions; artist-in-residence programs.
- **Public Exhibitions and Demonstrations:** Showcases of: student projects; prototypes; research outcomes; community innovations.
- **Regional Storytelling & Branding:** Narratives that highlight: local innovators; regional achievements; community impact; economic opportunity.
- **Community Convenings & Placemaking:** Events that bring residents together to build a shared sense of identity and belonging.

## FACILITIES

**Headline:** Facilities and Infrastructure Plan

**Current status (label column statement):** FSIC is a virtual organization located in New Smyrna Beach.
**Current status (rest):** A specific future site in Volusia County will be determined through a collaborative process with community members, civic leaders, industry partners, and other stakeholders. This ensures the final location reflects community priorities, long-term economic strategy, and the needs of partners.

> **Source:** fsic.space FAQ, "Where will FSIC be located?" (verbatim), split across the two columns.
> **Note:** This matches the site's position. The project lead decided (November 2026) that the site does not name a specific campus site or development. See `docs/08-current-site-audit.md` §3.3.

**Plan:** FSIC's facilities strategy is designed to scale with regional demand and support the phased deployment of the Talent, Innovation, Market, and Civic Engines. The infrastructure plan emphasizes flexibility, cost efficiency, and the creation of spaces that foster collaboration, hands-on learning, applied research, and community engagement.

**Guiding principles:**
- Flexible, modular spaces that adapt to evolving needs
- Co-location of education, industry, and community functions
- Accessibility for students, educators, employers, and residents
- Cost-effective phased build-out aligned with funding and demand
- Spaces that inspire creativity, collaboration, and civic pride

**Planned core spaces by phase** (phase names and timeframes as on the Deployment page, which links from here):
- **Phase 1: Foundation & Talent Engine Launch (Year 1):** classrooms and training labs for micro-credentials; flexible multipurpose space for workshops and events; small prototyping and fabrication area; office space for staff and partners; meeting rooms for employer and advisory council engagement
- **Phase 2: Innovation Engine Activation (Years 1–2):** challenge labs for student and industry collaboration; rapid prototyping studio with fabrication tools; small research and testing labs; collaboration zones for university and industry partners; expanded multipurpose space for innovation events
- **Phase 3: Market Engine Deployment (Years 2–3):** small business resource center; certification and compliance training rooms; startup incubation and co-working space; meeting rooms for procurement and contracting support; expanded prototyping and testing capacity
- **Phase 4: Civic Engine Expansion (Years 2–4):** public exhibition gallery; community event space; arts-and-space integration areas; STEM exploration zones for families and youth; outdoor gathering and placemaking spaces
- **Phase 5: Full Ecosystem Integration (Years 3–5):** full-scale innovation center; expanded research and prototyping labs; large multipurpose event and conference space; co-located university and industry partner suites; outdoor innovation plaza and community spaces
