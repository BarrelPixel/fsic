**New Smyrna Beach, FL** — The Florida Space Innovation Center (FSIC) is honored to welcome Jake Johansson to its Founders Council. Johansson’s decades of military service, his steady municipal leadership, and his deep experience in county-level governance have long contributed to the strength and resilience of communities across Volusia County. His commitment to public service and his collaborative leadership style bring valuable perspective to FSIC’s founding team.

Johansson served 35 years in the United States Navy, rising to the rank of Captain and holding multiple senior leadership and command roles. After retiring from military service, he continued serving the public as City Manager of Port Orange, where he guided one of Volusia County’s fastest-growing cities and strengthened operations, community engagement, and long-term planning. He later served on the Volusia County Council, working across jurisdictions to advance regional priorities, build partnerships, and support economic development efforts that benefit residents and businesses throughout the county.

His background in military command, municipal management, and county governance gives him a grounded understanding of how communities prepare for and benefit from Florida’s rapidly expanding space economy. Johansson brings a forward-looking perspective that aligns directly with FSIC’s mission to build a coordinated ecosystem for talent development, industry partnership, and regional collaboration. His insight will help guide FSIC through its early growth and future expansion.

FSIC is grateful to welcome Jake Johansson to the Founders Council and looks forward to the momentum his involvement will help drive across Southeast Volusia’s innovation landscape.
