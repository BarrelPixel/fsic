**Southeast Volusia, FL** — The Florida Space Innovation Center (FSIC) is proud to announce the appointment of Ryan Trine, a retired U.S. Army Chief Warrant Officer and nationally recognized cybersecurity and technology leader, to its Board of Directors.

With more than two decades of experience in national security, secure communications, enterprise technology, and cyber resilience, Trine brings a critical perspective to FSIC as the organization advances Southeast Volusia's role in Florida's growing aerospace and innovation economy. Throughout his military career, he served in several highly selective leadership assignments, including at the Pentagon and as Executive Communications Warrant Officer to the Commanding General of U.S. Army Forces Command (FORSCOM), where he led secure communications supporting one of the Army's largest operational commands.

"Ryan's experience represents the future of innovation leadership," said Erik Ahlgren, Board Chair of the Florida Space Innovation Center. "As aerospace, defense, artificial intelligence, and advanced manufacturing continue to converge, cybersecurity and resilient digital infrastructure become foundational to long-term success. Ryan brings an extraordinary depth of expertise that will help position FSIC as a leader in these emerging industries."

In addition to his distinguished military service, Trine has led enterprise technology modernization initiatives, managed complex information technology programs, and advised senior leaders on cybersecurity, operational resilience, and risk management. His academic background in homeland security and computer science, combined with numerous industry certifications, reflects a career dedicated to advancing technology while protecting the systems that support national and economic security.

As FSIC expands partnerships across aerospace, defense innovation, workforce development, education, and emerging technologies, Trine's leadership will strengthen the organization's efforts to build a resilient innovation ecosystem that attracts investment, develops high-skilled talent, and creates lasting economic opportunity throughout Southeast Volusia.

FSIC continues to assemble a diverse Board of Directors composed of leaders from government, education, business, defense, and technology who share a common vision of establishing Southeast Volusia as a premier hub for innovation and the future of Florida's space economy.

The Florida Space Innovation Center proudly welcomes Ryan Trine to its Board of Directors and looks forward to the expertise and leadership he will bring as the organization continues to shape the next chapter of innovation along Florida's Space Coast.
