## What will FSIC measure?

The **Talent Engine** is the part of FSIC that trains students and adults for careers in aerospace, advanced manufacturing, robotics and dual-use technologies. FSIC's Master Plan sets six metrics for it:

- **Number of students enrolled in micro-credentials:** how many people the short, job-focused training programs reach.
- **Completion and certification rates:** how many finish and earn the credential.
- **Dual-enrollment participation and credit attainment:** how many high school students take college courses through FSIC's STEM pathways, and how much college credit they earn.
- **Teacher upskilling participation:** how many educators take part in FSIC's professional development.
- **Internship, apprenticeship, and job placement rates:** how many participants move into work-based learning and jobs.
- **Employer satisfaction and alignment:** whether employers find that training matches what they need.

## How often are results reported?

The Master Plan sets three reporting lines for the Talent Engine:

- **Quarterly** program dashboards
- An **annual** workforce impact report
- **Employer advisory council** reviews

Talent Engine results also feed FSIC's wider public reporting, which the Master Plan lists as an Annual Impact Report, community presentations, grant-specific reporting for funders and regular updates to local governments and partners. The [Governance](/governance#reporting) page shows FSIC's full reporting framework.

## What principles guide the evaluation?

The Master Plan commits FSIC to "rigorous evaluation, transparent reporting, and continuous improvement," and lists five principles:

- Measure what matters most to employers, educators, funders and the community
- Use data to drive continuous improvement
- Report outcomes transparently and consistently
- Align metrics with state and federal funding requirements
- Demonstrate clear return on investment

## What has not been decided yet?

The Master Plan names what will be measured and how often results are reported. It does not set numerical targets, say which data sources will confirm employment, or define how long participants are followed after they finish. Those details are still to be set as programs are designed with partners.

## When will the first results come out?

The Talent Engine launches in Phase 1, which the Master Plan places in Year 1. Its expected outcomes for that phase include the first cohort of students enrolled in micro-credentials, employer-validated curriculum in place and teacher professional development under way. As of November 2026, FSIC is in Phase 1 and has not yet published program results.
