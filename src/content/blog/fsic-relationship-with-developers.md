## What role do developers play in FSIC's work?

FSIC's [FAQ](/faq/#developers) answers this in one sentence: "Developers play a collaborative — not controlling — role in FSIC's work."

FSIC describes what that collaboration can include: "Developers may help create spaces that support training, innovation, and community engagement, but FSIC does not endorse specific projects or drive development."

## Does FSIC take part in development decisions?

No. FSIC describes itself as an independent, community-aligned organization, and it states: "Large developments in the region have already been approved, and FSIC does not influence or promote those decisions."

FSIC also states that it "recognizes that many residents value slow growth or prefer no additional growth at all."

## What does FSIC do within approved growth?

FSIC's stated role is "to help ensure that what happens within that approved growth reflects community priorities by steering opportunities toward high-wage, future-focused industries rather than low-value or high-impact uses."

FSIC is designed to connect residents, students, educators, small businesses and employers to the space and advanced technology economy. It works through four engines: Talent, Innovation, Market and Civic, each described on [The FSIC Model](/model/) page. FSIC states that its focus is "protecting community character while shaping the future economy in ways that benefit the people who already live here."

## Where will FSIC be located?

FSIC's FAQ states: "FSIC is a virtual organization located in New Smyrna Beach." A future site in Volusia County "will be determined through a collaborative process with community members, civic leaders, industry partners, and other stakeholders." FSIC says this process "ensures the final location reflects community priorities, long-term economic strategy, and the needs of partners" ([FAQ](/faq/#location)).

## How is FSIC kept independent of any one interest?

FSIC's Master Plan states that "the governance model ensures that no single individual, partner, or sector can dominate decision-making" (p. 15). The board is designed to include an independent board chair, term limits, conflict-of-interest policies, balanced representation across sectors, and a clear separation between governance and operations (pp. 15–16).

The Master Plan's compliance framework also calls for conflict-of-interest disclosures and ethical fundraising policies (p. 17). These describe the governance FSIC is designed to have. FSIC is in its first phase, which includes establishing its governance, bylaws and board independence (p. 13).

FSIC's position on developers is also set out on the [Governance & Transparency](/governance/#developers) page, and how the board works is explained in [How Is FSIC Governed and Kept Independent?](/blog/how-fsic-is-governed/)
