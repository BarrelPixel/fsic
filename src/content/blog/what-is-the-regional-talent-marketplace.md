## What is the Regional Talent Marketplace?

The **Regional Talent Marketplace** is a digital platform planned by the Florida Space Innovation Center (FSIC). The Master Plan describes it as "a digital platform that connects" four groups:

- Students
- Job seekers
- Employers
- Educators

In the plan's words, "the marketplace provides visibility into regional opportunities and strengthens talent alignment." Those two sentences are most of what the plan says about it.

## Where does it fit in FSIC's plans?

The marketplace is one of the six core functions of FSIC's **Talent Engine**, the part of FSIC that prepares students and adults for careers in aerospace, advanced manufacturing, robotics and dual-use technologies. The others are micro-credential academies, dual-enrollment STEM pathways, industry-embedded learning, teacher upskilling, and K–12 STEM exploration. STEM stands for science, technology, engineering and mathematics.

The Talent Engine is the first part of FSIC to launch. The full program portfolio is on the [Programs & Facilities](/programs-facilities/#portfolio) page, and the Talent Engine is described on the [FSIC Model](/model/#talent) page.

## What problem is it meant to address?

The Master Plan does not tie a specific problem to the marketplace alone. For the Talent Engine as a whole, the problems it lists include "fragmented talent pipelines," "limited employer access to local talent" and "weak alignment between education and industry." Its regional need analysis also names "growing competition for talent from Brevard and Orlando."

The plan does not define "talent alignment" or say how the marketplace would achieve it.

## Who is it for?

The plan names the four groups the platform connects: students, job seekers, employers and educators. It also lists the "Regional Talent Marketplace for students" among the benefits school districts gain from working with FSIC.

For employers, the plan's partner benefits include "stronger talent pipelines" and "reduced hiring friction." It does not say how the marketplace itself would deliver them.

## When is it planned?

Phase 1, Foundation & Talent Engine Launch, is planned for Year 1. "Deploy the Regional Talent Marketplace" is one of its activities, and "Regional Talent Marketplace operational" is one of its expected outcomes. "Deploy Regional Talent Marketplace" is also among the Year 1 milestones in the plan's timeline.

As of November 2026, FSIC is in Phase 1, and the marketplace has not launched. The phases are on the [Phased Deployment](/deployment/#phase-1) page, and the programs planned first are in the FAQ answer [What kinds of programs will FSIC offer first?](/faq/#first-programs)

## How will FSIC measure it?

The Master Plan sets no metric for the marketplace by name. The Talent Engine's metrics include "internship, apprenticeship, and job placement rates" and "employer satisfaction and alignment." They are reported through quarterly program dashboards, an annual workforce impact report and employer advisory council reviews.

## What does the plan not say?

The Master Plan leaves most of the details open. It does not say:

- What the platform will do: for example, whether it will list jobs, internships or training, or match people to openings
- Who will build or run it
- What it will cost to build, or whether users will pay
- A launch date more precise than Year 1
- How it will handle users' personal information
- Which employers, schools or training providers will take part

None of these have been announced.
