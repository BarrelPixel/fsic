## Where do these numbers come from?

The figures in this post come from the **American Community Survey (ACS)**, a survey of households run by the U.S. Census Bureau. It asks about age, schooling, income and work. The estimates used here are the **2020–2024 5-year estimates**: answers collected from 2020 through 2024 and combined. For cities of this size, the Census Bureau publishes these tables only as 5-year estimates.

In this post, **Southeast Volusia** means three cities: New Smyrna Beach, Edgewater and Oak Hill. The figures are for people who live inside each city's limits. Volusia County and Florida are shown for comparison.

Two points apply to every table:

- **These are estimates.** The survey reaches a sample of households, so each figure has a margin of error. The Census Bureau publishes margins at a 90% confidence level. They are small for Florida and Volusia County and large for Oak Hill.
- **The percentages are calculated from the Census Bureau's counts.** Each one is a published count divided by another, such as residents aged 65 and over divided by all residents. Medians are the Census Bureau's own.

## How many people live in Southeast Volusia, and how old are they?

The three cities have 57,295 residents between them, 9.9% of Volusia County's population. New Smyrna Beach is the largest.

The **median age** is the age that divides a population in half: half the residents are older and half are younger.

Population and age, 2020–2024. The figures in brackets are margins of error.

| Place | Population | Median age | Aged 65 and over |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New Smyrna Beach | 31,974 | 57.8 | 35.3% |
| Edgewater | 23,627 | 53.7 | 33.1% |
| Oak Hill | 1,694 (±422) | 58.7 (±6.1) | 33.7% |
| Volusia County | 579,622 | 46.5 | 25.4% |
| Florida | 22,416,077 | 42.6 | 21.3% |

Residents of the three cities are older than residents of the county and the state. The median age in New Smyrna Beach is 57.8, which is 15.2 years above Florida's 42.6. Across the three cities, 34.3% of residents are 65 or older.

## How much schooling do residents have?

Educational attainment is the highest level of school a person has completed. The figures here are for people **aged 25 and over**. "High school diploma or higher" includes a GED or other equivalent.

Share of residents aged 25 and over, 2020–2024.

| Place | High school diploma or higher | Bachelor's degree or higher |
|---|---:|---:|
| New Smyrna Beach | 93.6% | 40.2% |
| Edgewater | 93.6% | 24.1% |
| Oak Hill | 88.4% | 16.8% |
| Volusia County | 92.1% | 28.4% |
| Florida | 89.9% | 34.1% |

About nine in ten adults in each place finished high school. The share with a bachelor's degree or higher varies more: 40.2% in New Smyrna Beach, above the state's 34.1%, and 24.1% in Edgewater, below it.

## What do households earn, and how many residents work?

**Median household income** is the income of the household in the middle: half of households have more and half have less. It counts the income of the people in the household over the past 12 months, in 2024 inflation-adjusted dollars.

The **labor force participation rate** is the share of residents aged 16 and over who are working or looking for work, or serving in the armed forces. The Census Bureau notes that people outside the labor force are mainly students, homemakers and retired workers.

Income and work, 2020–2024.

| Place | Median household income | Margin of error | In the labor force (aged 16 and over) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New Smyrna Beach | $83,865 | ±$5,575 | 51.9% |
| Edgewater | $60,977 | ±$4,546 | 52.1% |
| Oak Hill | $65,950 | ±$10,716 | 50.2% |
| Volusia County | $70,044 | ±$1,237 | 54.8% |
| Florida | $74,568 | ±$284 | 59.8% |

Median household income in New Smyrna Beach, $83,865, is above the state's $74,568. Edgewater's, $60,977, is below the county's $70,044. Oak Hill's margin of error is too wide to say whether its median is above or below the county's.

About half of residents aged 16 and over in each city are in the labor force, against 59.8% statewide. The rate counts everyone aged 16 and over, including retirees, and the three cities have a larger share of residents aged 65 and over than the state does.

## Which industries do residents work in?

The survey asks employed people what kind of business they work for and groups the answers into 13 industry sectors. The table shows each place's three largest sectors by share of employed residents, and the share who work in manufacturing. It describes the people who live in each place, wherever their job is.

Share of employed civilian residents aged 16 and over, 2020–2024.

| Place | Largest sector | Second | Third | Manufacturing |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| New Smyrna Beach | Education and health care, 21.3% | Professional, management and administrative services, 14.8% | Arts, entertainment, lodging and food service, 10.9% | 5.8% |
| Edgewater | Education and health care, 21.3% | Arts, entertainment, lodging and food service, 14.1% | Retail trade, 13.2% | 7.8% |
| Oak Hill | Professional, management and administrative services, 18.8% | Arts, entertainment, lodging and food service, 16.4% | Education and health care, 15.5% | 10.1% |
| Volusia County | Education and health care, 22.1% | Retail trade, 13.3% | Professional, management and administrative services, 13.1% | 5.9% |
| Florida | Education and health care, 21.0% | Professional, management and administrative services, 14.2% | Retail trade, 12.0% | 5.3% |

Education and health care is the largest sector for residents of New Smyrna Beach and Edgewater, as it is for the county and the state: about one employed resident in five works in it.

Read the order with care where sectors are close. In New Smyrna Beach, the third, fourth and fifth sectors are nearly the same size: arts, entertainment, lodging and food service (1,516 workers), construction (1,478) and retail trade (1,444). In Oak Hill, the survey estimates 676 employed residents in all, so each sector's count is small and the order could differ in the next survey.

The sector names are shortened here. The Census Bureau's full names are "Educational services, and health care and social assistance", "Professional, scientific, and management, and administrative and waste management services" and "Arts, entertainment, and recreation, and accommodation and food services".

## How precise are the figures for Oak Hill?

Less precise than the others, because Oak Hill is small. The table gives the Census Bureau's published estimates for Oak Hill and their margins of error. A margin of ±422 on a population estimate of 1,694 means the Census Bureau is 90% confident that the true number is between 1,272 and 2,116.

| Oak Hill, 2020–2024 | Estimate | Margin of error |
|---|---:|---:|
| Population | 1,694 | ±422 |
| Median age | 58.7 | ±6.1 |
| Median household income | $65,950 | ±$10,716 |
| Residents aged 16 and over in the labor force | 736 | ±200 |
| Employed residents | 676 | ±188 |
| Residents aged 25 and over with a bachelor's degree | 185 | ±77 |

Read Oak Hill's percentages as rough sizes, not exact values. For the larger cities the margins are narrower: New Smyrna Beach's median age of 57.8 has a margin of ±2.0 years, and Edgewater's 53.7 has a margin of ±3.4 years.

## How does this connect to FSIC?

These figures describe the community the Florida Space Innovation Center (FSIC) is being built to serve. The FSIC Master Plan describes FSIC as "designed to ensure that the growth of Florida's space economy directly benefits the people who live and work in Volusia County."

The Master Plan's concern is about what growth brings. It says Southeast Volusia "sits adjacent to one of the fastest-growing aerospace and advanced manufacturing corridors in the world, yet the region is not currently positioned to participate at scale," and warns of the region "absorbing population growth without capturing the high-wage jobs, employer investment, and economic mobility that should accompany it." [The Strategic Moment](/strategic-moment/) page sets out that case.

The survey figures are a baseline, not a forecast. They show who lives in the three cities and what work residents do in 2020–2024: 5.8% of employed residents of New Smyrna Beach and 7.8% of Edgewater's work in manufacturing, for example. They do not show what FSIC will change, and FSIC has made no projection for any measure in this post.

FSIC's programs are planned and not yet running; as of November 2026, FSIC is in Phase 1. The FAQ answer on [how FSIC will benefit local residents and families](/faq/#residents-and-families) lists what is planned for students, adults and the community, and the [Talent Engine](/model/#talent) section of the Model page describes the planned training. For the regional job market, see the post on [what aerospace and manufacturing jobs pay on the Space Coast](/blog/space-coast-aerospace-jobs-and-pay/).
